What does the AI voice assistant do? +

It is a voice-based AI assistant that can answer common questions, explain services, collect information from potential customers, and help direct people toward the next step. Each assistant is configured around the individual business.

Will I know when someone submits a lead? +

Lead notifications can be configured to send the business the information collected through its website or lead-capture system. The exact notification method depends on the project and integrations being used.

What is an AI video advertisement? +

I use modern AI-assisted production tools to create promotional videos, cinematic visuals, voiceovers, digital-avatar presentations, and other advertising assets. The appropriate tools depend on the project.

Do I own my website? +

Website ownership and ongoing hosting arrangements depend on the package selected. Before work begins, I'll clearly explain what is included, what is hosted by me, and what happens if you decide to move the site later.

How much does a project cost? +

Starting prices are shown above. More complicated projects may cost more depending on the amount of development, AI usage, video production, integrations, revisions, and other requirements. I'll provide the scope and price before beginning paid work.

How long does a video take? +

Production time varies depending on the concept, number of scenes, generation requirements, revisions, and final length. I'll provide an estimated delivery timeframe when quoting the project.
