What is the AI voice agent? +

It's a voice assistant built into your website that answers customer questions in real time — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Think of it like a receptionist who never sleeps, never misses a call, and alerts you instantly when a hot lead comes in. You can try it live at rooferagent.netlify.app — click the green button in the corner.

Will I know when a lead comes in? +

Yes — the moment someone fills out your estimate form, you get an instant email with all their details and a text message alert to your phone. No checking a dashboard, no delays. You find out the second it happens.

What is an AI Video Ad? +

Instead of hiring a full camera crew, I use advanced generative tools to create cinematic visuals, lifelike voiceovers, and even digital avatars to promote your business for a fraction of the cost. You can see an example in the Live Demo section above.

Do I own my website? +

Yes. For informational sites, I build them and hand them over so you never have to pay a monthly hosting fee to me. For AI-powered lead-capture sites, I offer a monthly retainer that includes hosting, maintenance, and ongoing support.

What social platforms do you manage? +

I can syndicate and schedule content across all major platforms including Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, and TikTok, depending on where your audience lives.

How long does a video take? +

Most short-form commercials and avatar videos can be scripted, generated, edited, and delivered within 1 to 2 weeks.
